# Jafna: a flattening swing raises low ground for stone, and nothing else costs any.
#
# Five claims:
#
#   1. Digging is free. A swing that pulls a mound down further than the hoe's one metre takes no
#      stone.
#   2. A swing with nothing to raise costs nothing and puts nothing in the middle of the screen.
#   3. Raising takes stone, and with enough of it the ground comes all the way up to the height the
#      swing aims at.
#   4. With no stone the ground comes up the hoe's own metre and stops there, about a metre short,
#      and the middle of the screen says "Not enough Stone".
#   5. With too little stone the swing raises the middle of the patch and bills only that: the one
#      stone leaves the pack, the middle comes up near the height, the middle of the screen says
#      "Not enough Stone", and nothing is left owing for the next swing.
#
# HOW A SWING AIMS ABOVE THE GROUND. A scenario cannot hold Left Alt and Jafna has no console
# command for a held height. It does not need either. Without Alt the hoe's Level ground does not
# aim at the height under the crosshair: Player.UpdatePlacementGhost puts the ghost where the
# crosshair is but at GetGroundHeight of your own position, and only AltPlace turns that off. So a
# swing taken standing on a mound two metres high aims two metres above the ground in front of it,
# and a swing taken from the ground at a mound aims below the mound. `build` presses the place
# button through the game's own path, so these are real swings with Jafna's patches and the stone
# in them, and `mark` reads the same ground height the ghost does.
#
# The continued flat cannot do this job. It is taken from the shaped points nearest the swing, and
# Raise ground and every hoe swing leave shaped points at the old height round their edge, so no
# swing at untouched low ground finds a flat above it.
#
# THE MOUNDS. Two Raise ground on one spot. raise_v2 was read out of the game files: radius 2, lift
# 1, power 0.1, Stone x2. That leaves a block about three metres square and 1.8 to 2 metres high
# that drops to the old ground within a metre. Mound B is 3 m ahead of where you start, mound A
# is 8 m behind you and mound C 20 m behind you. Swings from a mound are taken standing half a
# metre off its middle toward the spot, because from the middle, on some headings, the mound's own
# corner hides a spot three metres out.
#
# THE ORDER IS SET BY THE CREDIT, AND THE RUN NEEDS A FRESH SESSION. Jafna takes stone whole and
# keeps the rest until you log out, so a second run in the same session starts with up to a stone
# already paid for. This run used to spend that leftover itself, on its first swing, and since
# 2026-09-27 it cannot. A swing short of stone now raises whole points from the middle outward
# and stops at the first ring of points it cannot pay for in full, so part of a leftover survives
# a short swing. Carried into section 4's second half, it raises the middle of that swing, which
# is then measured as the ground a swing with no stone leaves, and a correct build fails there:
# about one re-run in six, worked through the copy of the game's arithmetic described below. So
# log out and back in before every run. With nothing carried in, nothing is paid for until
# section 5, which is why sections 4, 2 and 1 come first: they expect no stone to move, and stone
# already paid for would let a build that charges where it should not pass them. The first swing
# is one no stone can pay for: from mound B, with an empty pack and Crafting at 100 so it covers
# 12 metres, it wants about 7 cubic metres raised, and one stone buys 4.1 (Raise ground's Stone x2
# over the 8.27 cubic metres one Raise ground adds). It runs short, and that is claim 4's message.
# Claims 2 and 1 follow, then claim 4's height, then claim 5, then claim 3.
#
# Claim 5 comes after claim 4's height because it is the first swing that spends stone, and it
# leaves up to about a third of a stone paid for: a short swing stops at a whole ring of points,
# so it rarely uses the stone to the last crumb. That leftover is why claim 3 no longer checks
# that its first stone leaves the pack; whether it does now depends on the leftover. Claim 5's
# own `carrying Stone 0` is the proof that a fill takes stone, and claim 3's first swing becomes
# the proof that claim 5 left no debt: it needs well under a stone, so a correct build, holding
# one stone and the leftover, is never short there.
#
# Claim 4's height and claim 3 are on opposite sides of mound A on purpose. A swing that runs out
# leaves ground a metre up and flat, and the next swing on that spot continues that flat instead of
# your height, so stone given afterwards raises nothing there.
#
# WHAT A BROKEN BUILD READS, per key check:
#
#   - `centre Not enough Stone yes` after the first swing, and again in section 4's second half:
#     a build that raises without stone, or never raises, says nothing.
#   - `height 0 3 -0.9 0.38` in section 4: that swing ran out, so the ground stopped at the free
#     metre, about -0.9. A build that raised it anyway reads -0.45 to 0. So does a correct build
#     run twice in one session, which is what NEEDS is for.
#   - `centre Not enough no` in section 2, with nothing in the pack: a build that charged for lift
#     the free metre already covers says "Not enough Stone" here wherever the ground under the
#     swing dips a few centimetres below your feet, which is most ground. The same check after
#     section 3's one-stone swing catches it every time.
#   - `carrying Stone 3` in section 1: a build that priced digging like filling takes one and
#     reads 2. `height 3 0 1.15 0.4` next to it proves the swing did dig: an untouched mound reads
#     about 1.9.
#   - `carrying Stone 0` in section 5: the one stone can only go to the fill. A fill that took no
#     stone, or never ran, leaves 1.
#   - `centre Not enough Stone yes` in section 5: one stone buys 4.1 cubic metres and that swing
#     wants 4.5 to 10. A build that never raises, or raises for free, says nothing.
#   - `height 0 2.5 -0.2 0.25` in section 5: the middle of the swing reached about the height.
#     The build this rule replaced, which spread short stone evenly, reads about -0.45 at the
#     median there, so this catches it in about 4 runs of 10. No spot does better: the swing
#     eases the ground round its middle, so even a correct build reads down to -0.45 wherever the
#     grid puts the nearest vertex (2 runs in 1000 read just below it). Manual step 6 is the one
#     that tells the two apart every time.
#   - `centre Not enough no` after section 3's first swing: a build that raised only the middle
#     in section 5 but billed the whole patch took the one stone and still owes most of another,
#     so it runs short on this swing. It catches that about 5 runs in 6, and a build that raised
#     and billed the whole patch the same.
#   - `height 0 -2.5 0 0.25` at the end: three paid swings bring the patch to your height. A build
#     that stopped at the free metre reads about -0.9.
#   - `carrying Stone 0` and `height 3 0 1.9 0.4` after each pair of Raise ground are the fixture.
#     If either fails, nothing after it means anything.
#
# The geometry was checked against a copy of the game's own terrain arithmetic (RaiseTerrain,
# SmoothTerrain, the collision mesh's triangles) and Jafna's Flat.Target and fill, over random
# headings, positions and slopes up to 7 percent. On a correct build every check passed. A build
# that raises without stone, one that never raises, one that charges for digging and one that
# charges for the free metre each failed at least one. One thing it could not catch is the
# three centimetre floor on touch-ups, which is manual step 5. Nothing here has run in a game.
#
# Checked again on 2026-09-27 for the middle-first rule, with section 5 added, 300 runs from a
# fresh session on slopes up to 5 percent: a correct build failed one, at -0.4501 on the middle
# check. The even-spread build failed 133, a build that raised the middle and billed all of it
# 253, a build that raised and billed all of it 253, a build that never raises all 300. Section 5
# measures the middle and not the edge of the raised patch on purpose: at the edge the grid and
# the collision mesh move the reading by more than the builds differ. A taller mound would widen
# the gap, and by the same arithmetic a third Raise ground puts the spot past the five metres the
# placement ray reaches from your eye.
#
# What no step here can see: nothing aims more than eight metres above the ground, which needs a
# held height or a drop no mound can make, so the eight metre rule, its message and its panel
# line are manual step 7. And no step reads the build panel for a spot, because the panel follows
# where the camera looks and `build` turns the look back after each swing, so the short-of-stone
# panel lines are manual steps 2 and 6.
#
# NEEDS: a session nothing has been paid for in yet, so log out and back in before every run (see
# THE ORDER above). Singleplayer, English as the game language (the message names the item), and a
# strip of untouched ground no steeper than 1 in 20 with no ward from 12 m ahead of you to 26 m
# behind, 9 m wide on either side. Not near a base. Eat first: there are fourteen swings at 5
# stamina each. No held height (the panel must not say HOLDING). Jafna's Reach settings at their
# defaults, because the 12 metre swings rely on MaxRadius 6.
#
# It can fail with nothing wrong in two ways. A patch that crosses a zone boundary, where each
# zone decides the continued flat for itself. And a steep slope: between 5 and 7 percent a correct
# build failed 25 runs in 300, mostly at section 5, where the one stone paid for the whole swing
# and so it was not short. A second run in the same session used to be a third, and is now simply
# a run that did not meet NEEDS.
#
# LEAVES: three mounds, one with its near side dug about a metre, two patches a metre up where the
# stone ran out, one patch whose middle is raised to mound height with a ledge round it, one patch
# raised to mound height, dirt over all of it. Crafting is put back. Devkit empties the pack and
# hands back the test kit. Jafna keeps up to a stone of credit until you log out.
#
# MANUAL STEPS, for what no step can see. Log out and back in first, which empties what Jafna has
# carried over and makes step 1's line come again. A mound below is two Raise ground on one spot,
# as the scenario builds them.
#
#   1. Take the hoe out and select Level ground. Expected, once a session, Verbose or not:
#      "Raising ground is priced off 'raise_v2' ... raise radius 2,00, delta 1,00, power 0,10,
#      square False, so one swing adds 8,27 cubic metres to flat ground for Stone x2. That is
#      0,242 $item_stone per cubic metre."
#   2. Stand on a fresh mound with 10 Stone and aim at untouched ground 2 to 3 m out. Expected: a
#      panel line "Filling up to that height costs N Stone", N at least 1. Drop all but 1 Stone.
#      Expected, when N is more than 1: "You carry 1 Stone, this swing fills the middle for 1
#      Stone, all of it costs N Stone", the same N. Drop the last one. Expected: "You carry 0
#      Stone, not enough to fill any of it, all of it costs N Stone".
#   3. Carry 20 Stone. From low ground, aim at the top of a mound and press Left Alt. Walk to
#      untouched low ground and swing once. Expected: the middle of the patch comes up to the held
#      height and Stone leaves the pack. Press Left Alt again to let go.
#   4. Type `setkey NoBuildCost` in the console (it needs devcommands), stand on a fresh mound with
#      10 Stone and swing at untouched ground 2 to 3 m out. Expected: before the swing the panel
#      says "Filling up to that height is free in this world", the middle comes up to the mound's
#      height, and all 10 Stone are still there. `removekey NoBuildCost` afterwards.
#   5. Stand where section 3 stood (on mound A, half a metre to its left) with 20 Stone and keep
#      swinging at the patch it raised until the panel shows no cost line before a swing. Drop
#      every stone and swing twice more. Expected: neither swing puts anything in the middle of
#      the screen, and there is still no cost line.
#   6. Log out and back in. With Crafting at 60 or more and exactly 1 Stone, stand on a fresh mound
#      half a metre off its middle toward untouched ground and aim at that ground 2.5 m out.
#      Expected before the swing: "this swing fills the middle for 1 Stone" in the panel. Swing.
#      Expected: "Not enough Stone" in the middle of the screen, and the stone leaves the pack. A
#      patch two or three metres across in the middle of the swing comes up to within a few tenths
#      of the mound's height. The ground round it comes up about a metre and stops, so there is a
#      ledge at the patch's edge. A build that spreads the stone evenly leaves no ledge: the whole
#      patch comes up part of the way and none of it reaches the mound's height. One stone and not
#      two: the whole swing wants about 7 cubic metres and two stones buy 8.3, so with two it is
#      usually not short at all. On level ground one covers the middle and the four points round
#      it, about 3.2 of the 4.1 cubic metres it buys, and stops at the diagonals, which need 1.8
#      more.
#   7. Find a drop of more than nine metres onto flat ground nobody has shaped, a cliff or the side
#      of a tall rock. Press Left Alt with the crosshair on the top, go down, carry 20 Stone, and
#      swing at the flat ground at least six metres out from the foot of the drop. Expected before
#      the swing: no cost line, and "The ground here cannot be filled that high" in the panel.
#      Expected after it: "Too far below to raise" in the middle of the screen, all 20 Stone still
#      there, and the ground up about a metre. Press Left Alt again to let go.


# ---- setting up

clear
give Hoe:1,Stone:4
skill Crafting 0
tool Hoe mud_road_v2
wait 1

# Jafna's lines are in the build panel, nothing is being held, and the reach is the hoe's own
# three metres, which sections 1, 2 and 3 are measured at.
onscreen Flattens yes
onscreen HOLDING no
onscreen adds nothing yet yes

# The centre message stays in its text after it fades, so an old "Not enough" from earlier play
# would answer the first check below. This reads and clears it and cannot fail.
centre (clearing the message line) no


# ---- mound B, 3 m ahead

mark
build raise_v2 3 0
wait 1
build raise_v2 3 0
wait 1
carrying Stone 0
height 3 0 1.9 0.4


# ---- 4, first half: no stone, from mound B, 12 m wide, so it cannot be paid for

wait 2
move 3.5 0
skill Crafting 100
wait 1
onscreen up from the hoe's own yes

build mud_road_v2 2.5 0
wait 1
centre Not enough Stone yes
skill Crafting 0


# ---- 2: nothing to raise, from the ground where you started, 3.5 m to the right

wait 2
move -3.5 0
build mud_road_v2 0 3.5
wait 1
centre Not enough no


# ---- 1: digging is free, at mound B's near side

give Stone:3
build mud_road_v2 1.5 0
wait 1
carrying Stone 3
centre Not enough no
height 3 0 1.15 0.4


# ---- mound A, 8 m behind where you started

wait 2
move -11 0
clear
give Hoe:1,Stone:4
tool Hoe mud_road_v2
wait 1
mark
build raise_v2 3 0
wait 1
build raise_v2 3 0
wait 1
carrying Stone 0
height 3 0 1.9 0.4


# ---- 4, second half: no stone, measured, from mound A's right side
#
# Nothing is carried over any more, so the swing raises nothing past the hoe's metre. 12 m wide
# again, because then the metre it does raise is flat round the spot, and a fill would have left
# a dish there, so the two cannot read alike at the measured point half a metre further out.
#
# Half a metre out is also exactly where a leftover credit lands, and it stays there anyway.
# Further out, beyond what less than a stone can pay for, a fill barely shows either: the hoe eases
# with power 1, so 2.5 m from the middle a build that raises without stone reads about -0.8,
# inside this check. Run through the copy of the game's arithmetic, `height 0 5` let a build that
# raises without stone and bills nothing pass all 300 runs; this check fails it in all 300.

wait 2
move 3 0.5
mark
skill Crafting 100
build mud_road_v2 0 2.5
wait 1
centre Not enough Stone yes
height 0 3 -0.9 0.38
skill Crafting 0


# ---- mound C, 20 m behind where you started

wait 2
move -15 -0.5
give Stone:4
mark
build raise_v2 3 0
wait 1
build raise_v2 3 0
wait 1
carrying Stone 0
height 3 0 1.9 0.4


# ---- 5: one stone, short, measured, from mound C's right side
#
# The same swing as section 4's second half, 12 m wide from the mound, with one stone in the pack.
# It wants 4.5 to 10 cubic metres and the stone buys 4.1, so it is always short. It raises the
# middle point and the rings round it that the stone pays for in full, takes the stone, and leaves
# the rest to the hoe's free metre. Measured at the spot, which is the middle of the swing.

wait 2
move 3 0.5
mark
skill Crafting 100
give Stone:1
build mud_road_v2 0 2.5
wait 1
carrying Stone 0
centre Not enough Stone yes
height 0 2.5 -0.2 0.25
skill Crafting 0


# ---- 3: raising takes stone and reaches your height, from mound A's left side
#
# One stone first, on top of whatever section 5 left paid for. This swing needs well under a
# stone's worth and more than nothing, so on a correct build it is never short, and here that is
# the check that section 5 left nothing owing. Whether this stone leaves the pack depends on that
# leftover, so it is not checked. Then five more and two swings on the same spot, because one
# swing only lands its middle on your height and eases the ground round it.

wait 2
move 12 -1
mark
give Stone:1
build mud_road_v2 0 -2.5
wait 1
centre Not enough no

give Stone:5
build mud_road_v2 0 -2.5
wait 1
build mud_road_v2 0 -2.5
wait 1
centre Not enough no
height 0 -2.5 0 0.25


skill Crafting back
